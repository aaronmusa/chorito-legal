Reporting a problem

The more of this you can include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • What you were doing, and what you expected instead.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings › General › About).
  • The Chorito version, shown in the app’s settings.
  • Whether you’re a manager, co-manager or member of the household.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

Common questions

How do I join my household? Ask the household’s manager for the invite code and enter it after signing in.

Why hasn’t my chore been approved? A manager or co-manager has 24 hours from when you mark it done to approve or reject it. If nobody decides in that time, it approves itself and the points are yours. Nobody can review their own work.

Why did I lose points? When you take a chore from the pool you pick how many days you need, from 1 to 7. If the window passes and the chore isn’t done, its points come off your total and it goes back to the pool. You can ask for it again straight away.

Can I give a pool chore back? Yes. Releasing it before your window ends costs nothing.

Which day does Chorito think it is? The household’s day, in the household’s timezone, not your phone’s. Chores, the review window and the monthly board all follow it.

How do I turn notifications off? Two are optional: the morning digest and “anyone finishes a chore”. Everything else is about you or something waiting on you, so it stays on. You can silence Chorito entirely in iOS Settings › Notifications.

How do I delete my account? From the app’s settings, or email me. The Privacy Policy explains what is removed.
